Delete Account & Data — Rent & Play

App: Rent & Play (com.rentandplay.rent_and_play) · Developer: altGroup / Rent & Play · Support: rentandplay0@gmail.com

Option 1: Delete Directly Inside the Rent & Play App (Immediate)

  1. Open the Rent & Play app and sign in to your account.
  2. Ensure you have no active or pending rentals (complete or cancel open rentals first).
  3. Go to Profile → Settings → Delete account & data and confirm Delete account.

Option 2: Request Account Deletion Without the App

If you no longer have the Rent & Play app installed, submit your deletion request from your registered email address so we can verify account ownership and process deletion within 7 days.

Or send an email directly to rentandplay0@gmail.com with subject Account Deletion Request - Rent & Play from your registered email address.

What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

  • Deleted: Your Firebase Authentication login credentials, profile record (name, email, phone number, profile photo), push notification device tokens, and support chat preferences are permanently purged or anonymized.
  • Retained (where required): Historical completed payment ledger and equipment condition records without active personal access may be retained in anonymized accounting form for equipment damage auditing and dispute settlement.
