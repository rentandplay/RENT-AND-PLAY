1. Information We Collect

Rent & Play collects only the personal and transaction information required to operate equipment rentals and protect inventory:

  • Account & Profile Details: Full name, email address, optional mobile phone number, and optional profile photo.
  • Rental & Payment Records: Equipment reservations, pickup/return timestamps, rental extension requests, equipment inspection notes, and optional uploaded payment proof screenshots.
  • Notification & Session Identifiers: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) device tokens used to deliver rental approval, return, and due-time alerts.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to authenticate your account (including Email/Password and Google Sign-In), process equipment rental bookings, verify QR handoffs and returns, manage rental extensions and payments, and deliver real-time rental notifications. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data or serve third-party advertisements.

3. Device Permissions

  • Camera & Photos / Media: Requested only when scanning equipment or ticket QR codes, uploading optional payment proof screenshots, or updating your profile photo.
  • Notifications: Used to send real-time rental approval, extension, inspection, and return alerts.
  • Bluetooth / Nearby Devices (Staff Devices): Used optionally by authorized staff to connect to portable Bluetooth thermal receipt and QR label printers. On Android 11 and earlier, legacy Bluetooth discovery permission is used strictly for printer pairing and never for GPS location tracking.

4. Data Storage & Security

Account and rental records are transmitted over HTTPS/TLS and stored using Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase Cloud Messaging, and Supabase Storage with role-based access controls.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You may permanently delete your account and personal profile data directly inside the mobile app under Settings → Delete account & data (after completing or cancelling any open rentals), or request deletion online at any time via our Account Deletion page or by emailing rentandplay0@gmail.com.
